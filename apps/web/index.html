run-far

run-far is a personal training dashboard for runners. It tracks how recovered you are each day using Whoop recovery data, helps you build a running plan around that, and keeps the plan on your Google Calendar — so the run you do today matches the shape you're actually in.

How run-far uses your Google account

  • Google Calendar — writes your planned runs to a dedicated "Running" calendar, and reads your primary calendar only to flag conflicts.
  • Gmail (send only) — emails your recovery digest to you. It never reads your existing mail.
  • Google sign-in — your name and email identify your run-far account.

run-far is built and operated by a single developer, Teddy Grace, and access is invite-only. Privacy policy
